Mystery Forest Studio ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile game Mystery Forest (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you play our Game.

We do not directly collect any personal information from you. All game progress (coins, level, items, etc.) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

3. Third-Party Services

Our Game uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google AdMob - For displaying advertisements.

These services may collect:

4. How We Use Information

The information collected by third-party services is used solely for:

  • Displaying relevant advertisements

5. Children's Privacy

Our Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

6. Data Security

We do not store personal data on our servers. Game progress is stored locally on your device. Third-party services have their own security measures.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt out of personalized advertising (via device settings)
  • Reset your Advertising ID (via device settings)
  • Uninstall the Game at any time
